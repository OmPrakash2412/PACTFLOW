# PACTFLOW Hackathon Demo Script (Under 3 Minutes)

**Presenter Note**: Speak with confidence, focusing on the seamless loop:
`AGREEMENT → WORK → EVIDENCE → APPROVAL → PAYPAL PAYMENT → TRUST LEDGER`.

---

### [0:00 - 0:35] The Problem & The Solution
> "Good day judges. Digital freelance work today is broken across chaotic chat threads, ambiguous requirements, and disconnected payments. Clients don't know if deliverables actually work, and freelancers fear unpaid work.
> 
> Welcome to **PACTFLOW: Where Work Meets Trust.**
> PACTFLOW is an AI-powered agreement, deliverable verification, and PayPal milestone payment platform that replaces ambiguity with cryptographic accountability."

---

### [0:35 - 1:15] Step 1 & 2: Gemini Pact Architect & Dual Acceptance
> *(Show Landing Page and navigate to Client Hub)*
> "Here on the Client Hub, a client creates a project. Rather than vague text, **Gemini Pact Architect** synthesizes requirements and total budget into a legally structured agreement with concrete, objective acceptance criteria and milestone budget allocations.
> 
> Both the client and the freelancer digitally sign the agreement version. This seals our first cryptographic event block in the **Trust Ledger** using SHA-256."

---

### [1:15 - 1:55] Step 3 & 4: Evidence Submission & Gemini Evidence Inspector
> *(Switch to Freelancer View, open Milestone 2, and inspect deliverable)*
> "The freelancer works on the milestone and submits code repositories, test logs, and staging URLs.
> Immediately, our **Gemini Evidence Inspector** performs a rigorous audit. Notice: the AI uses honest assessments: *satisfied*, *uncertain*, or *missing*. 
> Crucially, static screenshots are never accepted as proof of dynamic code or servers. 
> And our fundamental rule: **AI never approves or authorizes payouts directly**—it produces an advisory audit for the human client."

---

### [1:55 - 2:35] Step 5 & 6: Client Approval & Real PayPal Sandbox Settlement
> *(Show client approval button and click 'Generate PayPal Sandbox Invoice')*
> "The client reviews the AI report and approves the deliverable.
> Now, the backend invokes the **PayPal Developer Sandbox Invoicing API v2**. It creates and sends a real invoice, capturing PayPal's hosted checkout URL.
> 
> The client pays through PayPal's official hosted experience. PayPal transmits an authoritative webhook with cryptographic transmission headers. 
> Our backend authoritatively verifies the signature, prevents duplicate deliveries through idempotency checks, marks the milestone paid, and seals the payment in the Trust Ledger."

---

### [2:35 - 3:00] Conclusion & The Trust Ledger
> *(Navigate to Trust Ledger tab)*
> "Finally, look at the **Trust Ledger**. Every single event—from the genesis block to final PayPal settlement—is chained with SHA-256 hashes. Tamper-evident, zero escrow ambiguity, completely verifiable.
> 
> That is **PACTFLOW: Where Work Meets Trust.** Thank you."
